How to read this map

Temperature is a satellite estimate of the surface, not air temperature or a health-risk score. Thermal detail is about 100 m, although the delivered pixels are 30 m.

Green pixel share is the fraction of matched clear-land pixels with a vegetation signal (NDVI ≥0.30). It is a provisional estimate, not a tree inventory.

Uncertainty is the Landsat ST_QA estimate. Higher values mean less certainty. Reported cell values are medians, not confidence intervals.

We exclude water candidates, a 60 m coastal buffer and crop-edge halo, uncertain land, cloud flags and temperature uncertainty above 2 K. Historical WorldCover water, wetland and mangrove classes are also excluded conservatively. Limited OSM reference checks have mixed results: water tags can conflict with the current image, and tidal vegetation complicates shoreline labels. No coastal reference point enters the retained thermal comparison. These checks do not establish accuracy.

6 September was excluded: none of the historical urban inland support passed the strict QA limit. Date acceptance requires at least 50% coverage of that support. Sentinel greenery is from 15 September for the 14 September thermal scene, and from 30 September for the other scene.

Buildings come from contributed OSM closed ways. Historical built-up land comes from the machine-learning-derived ESA WorldCover 2021 product. We do not run our own AI building segmentation.

Priorities combine relative ranks for surface heat (50%), low green signal (30%) and mapped buildings (20%). We tested 13 settings, including urban screening cutoffs. Reported thermal uncertainty does not capture every possible error.
