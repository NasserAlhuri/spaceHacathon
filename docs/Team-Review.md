## Questions every member should be able to answer

1. What does the PoC actually implement? Satellite indicators, contributed geometry and upstream ML land cover; our own VHR segmentation remains future work.
2. What does the temperature mean? Satellite-observed surface temperature, not air temperature or health risk.
3. Why a 300 m cell? To aggregate screened, spatially dependent thermal support; fine property-level conclusions are not justified.
4. Why are there 13 scenarios? To expose rank sensitivity. No uniquely verified best intervention site is claimed.
5. What remains to validate? The study boundary, contributed references, candidate-site suitability, field conditions and end-user benefit.
