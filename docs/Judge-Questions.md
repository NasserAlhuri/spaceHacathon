These answers describe the published proof of concept. They are preparation material, not completed member reviews. Keep the map's Unknown fields and all stated limitations visible.

| Question | Suggested answer |
|---|---|
| What decision does this support? | Our proposed user is a municipal planning team choosing where to inspect first. The next step checks use, shade, access, ownership and feasibility. No customer, adoption or measured benefit is confirmed. It combines relative satellite indicators with local context; it does not approve an intervention or measure human heat risk. |
| Where is the AI? | We use externally produced AI land-cover data: WorldCover in the heat-screening workflow and Dynamic World in a separate historical experiment. Our quality checks and ranking are rule based. We trained no new model. Dynamic World does not feed the original score: its primary common coverage is 30.94%, below the unchanged 50% gate. It does not establish accepted urban expansion, individual-tree/shade detection, health prediction or causal cooling. |
| Does 50.4°C mean people breathe air at that temperature? | No. It is the mean of two date-level surface-temperature medians for a 300 m cell. It is neither air temperature nor a stop-level or personal-exposure measurement. |
| Why use 300 m cells when delivered Landsat pixels are 30 m? | Native thermal detail is about 100 m, and delivered samples are spatially dependent. Aggregating screened support avoids implying fine property-level temperature precision. It still cannot isolate a bus stop or individual tree. |
| Did you visit the sites? What did you measure? | Nasser's original visit dates remain unspecified. He separately confirms Abdulrahman visited the three photographed sites on 8 October 2026 and captured all received media in a shared 13:00–14:00 Qatar (UTC+3) window, not EXIF; individual times remain Unknown. Abdulrahman's qualitative observations are attributed. October photographs are later context for September thermal data. User counts, use times and detailed shade assessment were not measured. Visits do not constitute independent thermal validation. |
| Why does the map still say Unknown? | No evidence establishes those particular use, ownership or planned-use details. Confirming a visit does not fill those gaps. We preserve Unknown until a documented observation or measurement supports a change. |
| How can the bus-stop cell have zero greenery when there are trees? | Zero is the share detected under NDVI ≥0.30 on matched clear-land pixels, not a tree inventory. The pixel audit reproduces zero at that threshold, with lower-threshold signal present. Sparse or small trees can be poorly represented in the cell-scale signal. |
| Why only two temperature dates? | The 14 and 30 September 2026 scenes pass the coverage and uncertainty checks. September sixth fails: none of the historical urban inland support passed the strict QA limit. Two accepted snapshots cannot establish a seasonal or long-term trend. |
| How do you handle clouds and the coast? | QA screens cloud flags, uncertainty above 2 K, water and uncertain land; it also excludes a 60 m coastal buffer and crop-edge halo. Historical water, wetland and mangrove classes are conservatively excluded. Compare the common inland footprints retained on both dates. |
| Is the study boundary the whole official city? | No. The 51.445–51.555°E, 25.635–25.730°N window is analyst-defined and includes urban, desert and coastal context. Official-boundary and stakeholder validation remain pending. |
| How is priority calculated? | It combines relative ranks: heat 50%, low green signal 30%, mapped buildings 20%. Urban candidates require historical built-up coverage ≥10% or mapped buildings ≥2%. These choices can miss recent or unmapped development. |
| What do the thirteen scenarios prove? | They expose sensitivity to weights and screening settings. Rank ranges are not confidence intervals and do not establish a uniquely best intervention. The highlighted sites remain investigation candidates. |
| Are your reference checks an accuracy score? | No. NDVI >=0.30 at 24/25 grass reference points and 0/30 building-labelled reference points. The 0/30 result is a vegetation-threshold diagnostic on building references, not zero building-detection accuracy. Separately, historical WorldCover built class 50 agrees at 25/30 building reference points. All 30 coastal references are excluded from retained thermal support. These use contributed labels, QA/rule checks and a historical product; none is independently measured overall accuracy. Contributed labels and shoreline context can conflict; independent validation remains pending. |
| Would planting trees definitely cool these locations? | We did not estimate causal cooling or design interventions. Shade, shelters, trees and safer routes need site measurements, ownership/access checks, engineering assessment and stakeholder review. |

A future planning-support service is a hypothesis, not a business result. No partnership, paid pilot, revenue, time saving, cooling or health effect is measured.
